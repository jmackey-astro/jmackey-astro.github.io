# Jonathan Mackey

I am a scientific researcher at the University of Cologne, 1st Physics Institute, in the [Theoretical Astrophysics Group](https://astro.uni-koeln.de/computational-astrophysics).  My work is mainly theoretical/computational, investigating shocks and radiation in ionised plasmas, and high-energy processes.  I am also involved in a number of international observing collaboration.  This is my personal webpage with links to information that I find useful.
